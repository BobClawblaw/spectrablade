# Roadmap

What comes next, in order. Finished work moves to the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

1. **DTMF and other tone sequences** in Generate.
2. **Pitch correction:** to a scale automatically, and by hand along a drawn pitch curve.
3. **5.1 the whole way:** track effects on all six channels of a 5.1 track, a master rack past left and right, and freezing 5.1 tracks.
4. **Scrubbing a session:** the mix heard as the mouse drags over the session's timeline (files are scrubbed since milestone 248).

Not code: Reduce Reverb and Level Speech are yet to be tried on real recordings and listened to, and a Developer ID (for notarizing) is the remaining step before installing opens without Privacy & Security.
