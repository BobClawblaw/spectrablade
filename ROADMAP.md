# Roadmap

What comes next, in order. Finished work moves to the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

1. **A six-channel master rack:** the rack carrying a 5.1 file's or 5.1 session's channels together while it plays (and a file's when applied), so its compressor and limiter can link them too (now each pair goes through copies of its own).
2. **A fuller Pitch Editor:** splitting and joining notes, moving notes in time, keeping formants on large shifts, and editing session clips.
3. **7.1:** a 7.1 master and 7.1 tracks, as 5.1 has them.
4. **Automation in track templates:** templates keep the track's envelopes.
5. **Effects while scrubbing a session:** the effects that can follow a head going backwards (no latency, no memory) heard while scrubbing.
6. **Pitch and time editing of session clips:** the Pitch Editor and stretching on clips in place.
7. **Loudness to a delivery spec:** one step to meet EBU R128 or a streaming target, with a report.

Not code: Reduce Reverb and Level Speech are yet to be tried on real recordings and listened to, and a Developer ID (for notarizing) is the remaining step before installing opens without Privacy & Security.
