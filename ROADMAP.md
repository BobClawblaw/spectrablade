# Roadmap

What comes next, in order. Finished work moves to the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

1. Restoration: repairing clipped peaks.
2. Generating audio: tones, noise and silence of a length, into the file at the cursor.
3. Spectral editing by hand: a lasso and brush in the spectrogram, and healing a spot from what surrounds it.
4. Multitrack routing: a track's output to a chosen bus or hardware pair, sidechain into a compressor, and ducking under a voice.
5. Files: MP3 and AAC export, and keeping BWF and ID3 details.
