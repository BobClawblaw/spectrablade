# Roadmap

What comes next, in order. Finished work moves to the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

1. **Centre channel extract and remove:** what is panned to the middle of a stereo mix (voice, dialogue), taken out or kept alone, band by band.
2. **Automatic phase correction:** finding and taking out a time offset between channels.
3. **DTMF and other tone sequences** in Generate.
4. **Pitch correction:** to a scale automatically, and by hand along a drawn pitch curve.
5. **5.1 the whole way:** track effects on all six channels of a 5.1 track, a master rack past left and right, and freezing 5.1 tracks.
6. **Scrubbing a session:** the mix heard as the mouse drags over the session's timeline (files are scrubbed since milestone 248).

Not code: Reduce Reverb and Level Speech are yet to be tried on real recordings and listened to, and a Developer ID (for notarizing) is the remaining step before installing opens without Privacy & Security.
