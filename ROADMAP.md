# Roadmap

What comes next, in order. Finished work moves to the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

1. **7.1 tracks:** tracks that play 7.1 clips straight to a 7.1 master's eight channels, as 5.1 tracks do (7.1 files since milestone 263, a 7.1 master since 264).
2. **Automation in track templates:** templates keep the track's envelopes.
3. **Effects while scrubbing a session:** the effects that can follow a head going backwards (no latency, no memory) heard while scrubbing.
4. **Pitch and time editing of session clips:** the Pitch Editor and stretching on clips in place.
5. **Loudness to a delivery spec:** one step to meet EBU R128 or a streaming target, with a report.

Not code: Reduce Reverb and Level Speech are yet to be tried on real recordings and listened to, and a Developer ID (for notarizing) is the remaining step before installing opens without Privacy & Security.
